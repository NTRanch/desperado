# Desperado

A space-western arena-duel game for **GitHub Game Off 2026**.
One town, one night, one Form.

Ten years ago the Adler family of gunsmiths was murdered, and their
youngest was framed for it. The family secret — the **Desperado**, a
magic-tech gun that changes shape with its wielder's desire — was
supposed to die with them. Tonight, in the orbital desert town of
San Aurelia, the heir comes back to settle it: three duels between
dusk and dawn against the Morrow Combine, the arms conglomerate
that profits from perpetual war and killed to own the family's
technique.

## The game (jam cut)

- **One place:** San Aurelia — a single town block (plaza, family
  workshop, water-tower sightline) visited three times across one
  night: dusk, midnight, dawn. The same map changes state each pass.
- **One mechanic: La Forma** — the family's kata ("the Form": a kata
  is a form, and the gun changes form). A last-instant dodge or
  parry earns a Beat; Beats morph the Desperado mid-combo between
  three desires: **Vengeance** (dual pistols → Gatling pressure),
  **Truth** (rifle shots that expose weak points and hidden
  caches), **Freedom** (burst movement and route-opening).
- **Depth through replay:** six hidden Deseo caches, alternate
  duel openings, a no-kill boss route, four short endings, a
  speedrun timer, and a New Game+ "High Noon" remix.
- **Shape:** 25–35 minutes for a first clear. Stylised 2D/2.5D
  presentation, sunset-to-dawn palette passes.
