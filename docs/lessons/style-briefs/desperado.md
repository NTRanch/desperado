# Desperado — style brief

**The shape.** One town block (San Aurelia) over one night, visited
at dusk / midnight / dawn; one mechanic — La Forma, the gun that
morphs in three expressions (Vengeance, Truth, Freedom); three
duels + a three-phase boss; six hidden Deseo caches; four endings.
Godot 4, 2D, 25–35 minute first clear. Submissions close
**2026-12-01 13:37 PST** — this brief's game is the SFW lane's only
hard-deadline title.

**Art consequences.**

- The town block is texture/plate work in THREE pass states
  (dusk/midnight/dawn) — palette-and-light re-grades of one block,
  not three paintings (kit discipline, environments pack).
- Characters are 2D sprites + portraits: protagonist, 3 duelists,
  the boss, the false-witness NPC set. The duelists and boss are
  characters with duel-state variants, not an enemy class.
- La Forma's three morph expressions are the signature object set:
  one gun, three readable silhouettes; the shift-snap effect
  (effect-timeline data for the Godot build) carries morphs, pass
  transitions, and boss phases.
- Western attire with per-pass palette/state variants.

**Media discipline (the design's own law).** Every generated
keeper/reject records: prompt, model/settings, seed (DESIGN.md) —
the keeper-chain discipline of the art bible, already native to
this title's workflow. Generation is the working method here, and
its log is the provenance.
