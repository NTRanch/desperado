# Desperado — design (jam cut)

Lead concept for GitHub Game Off 2026, cut to the jam's winning
formula: one strong mechanic, one compact space, depth through
secrets/replay/mastery, polish everywhere. The larger adventure
RPG this premise could grow into is explicitly out of scope for
the jam build.

## Fiction

- Protagonist: the Adler heir, framed at 19 for the mass murder
  of his family by an agent of the Morrow Combine, a corporate
  arms conglomerate that arms all sides for profit and wanted to
  eliminate independent gunsmiths who would not fall in line.
- The family's art: hybrid magic gunsmithing that creates
  **Deseos** — guns aligned to their smith's deepest desire,
  powered by strength of will.
- The treasured secret: **the Desperado**, a magic-tech gun that
  fluidly changes shape — dual pistols, Gatling gun, rifle,
  scatter/rocket forms — wielded through gun-kata martial arts.
- Mission: return to San Aurelia and face the framing agent.

## Structure

- Three passes over one town block: dusk, midnight, dawn.
- Three duels: Combine enforcer, false witness, framing agent
  (boss, three phases — each phase answers one morph and
  punishes one-note play).
- Short investigation loops between duels in the same plaza;
  one reused flashback room (the workshop, ten years ago),
  re-lit and re-voiced per pass.
- Endings (short tableau + a few lines): Revenge, Truth,
  Freedom, Release — decided by the final desire stance plus
  secrets found.

## La Forma (the mechanic)

- Last-instant dodge/parry earns a **Beat**.
- Beats are spent to morph the Desperado **mid-combo**.
- Morphs are expressions of one system, not three systems:
  Vengeance (pressure), Truth (exposure), Freedom (position).
- The player's skill is choosing the desire that fits the
  moment while keeping the kata flowing.

## Cut list (explicit)

No open world, no party, no crafting, no inventory economy, no
skill tree, no second town, no full action-RPG system stack,
and no full 3D non-photorealistic rendering inside the jam
month. Those remain north-star references for a later
Desperado, not this entry.

## Media-generation experiment

Every generated keeper/reject records: prompt, model/settings,
what was kept, and what the iteration fixed (readability, morph
telegraphs, character consistency). Audio is produced silently
and gated by the Owner's ear.
